# Quiz Wizard Tutorial

Welcome to **Quiz Wizard**, a desktop quiz game that challenges your knowledge across several domains.

## Main Menu

- **Play** — enter your name and age, choose a category and difficulty, then answer multiple-choice questions.
- **Leaderboard** — view high scores ranked from highest to lowest.
- **Tutorial** — show this help text.
- **Exit** — close the application.

## Categories

1. General Knowledge  
2. Technical  
3. Geopolitical  

## Difficulty Levels

1. **Easy** — easier question bank  
2. **Medium** — moderate challenge  
3. **Hard** — toughest bank  
4. **Auto** — starts on Easy and adapts as you play  

## Scoring

Each correct answer awards **10** points. Wrong answers award **0**. There is no timer and no lives system. Question order follows the bank order.

## Auto Mode

- Starts on the Easy bank for your chosen category.
- **Three correct answers in a row** raise difficulty (Easy → Medium → Hard).
- **Three wrong answers in a row** lower difficulty (Hard → Medium → Easy).
- When difficulty changes, streaks reset and play continues at the **same question index** in the new bank.
- The last three results are shown while you play (✓ / ✗).

## Leaving early

Press **Esc** or **Quit** during a quiz to finish early. Your score so far is still saved to the leaderboard.

## Tips

- Read each explanation after you answer — it helps on later runs.
- Names may include spaces; age must be a whole number between 1 and 120.
